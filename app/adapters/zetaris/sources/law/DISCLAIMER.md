DISCLAIMER
==========

Limits of the law documents in this folder.

This folder holds copies of Malaysian law texts. It is a reference collection, not a legal service.


WHAT THIS IS FOR
----------------

Prepared for a hackathon prototype. It supports a demonstration of an AI agent that reads recorded law text.

Use is limited to that purpose:

  - internal reference, during the build and the demonstration;
  - a non-commercial project;
  - no redistribution of the source PDFs.

The pdf/ folder holds the source copies and is not published. Only the derived metadata is intended to be shared: this file, README.md, NOTICE.md, LICENSE, pdf_manifest.json, the json/ text, and the provenance pages.

Nothing here is for reliance. No exemption, permission, or licence is claimed beyond what is stated in LICENSE and NOTICE.md.


NOT THE OFFICIAL TEXT
---------------------

These are files downloaded from websites. They are not copies of the Gazette printed by the Government Printer.

Interpretation Acts 1948 and 1967 (Act 388) s.61 gives a presumption of authenticity to copies of the Gazette printed by the Government Printer. A file downloaded from a website is not such a copy.

These files are evidence of the text of the instrument. They are not authenticated copies.


CHECK THE DATE
--------------

Every document carries a revision date, `law_as_at`. The law may have changed since that date.

  P.U.(A) 460/2003   in force 1 January 2004   Act 127            as at 1 January 2006     <-- stale, later amendments not applied   Act 341            as at 1 October 2009   Act A1712          in force 7 July 2024

Do not cite any document without its date. A citation without a revision date cannot be checked.


NOT COMPLETE
------------

Only 4 documents are held. Other laws and guidance that apply to open burning are not here. In particular:

  - The 2026 amendment to Act 127 (A1793) is not held.
  - The Occupational Safety and Health Act 1994 (Act 514) is not held.
  - No DOSH or MOH guidance documents are held.
  - Act 127 is the 2006 reprint, not a consolidated version.

This folder must not be read as a complete account of the law on open burning. The full list of documents left out, and why, is in pdf_manifest.json under "excluded_from_ingest".

Nothing in this folder is advice, an opinion, or a conclusion about what the law means or what it requires.

No legal advice is given. Nothing here replaces a qualified lawyer.


WHAT YOU CAN DO
---------------

  - Read the text, and check it against the source link in pdf_manifest.json.
  - Re-derive the json/ folder and confirm the SHA-256 hashes match.
  - Quote the text together with its instrument code and its revision date.

See README.md for what each file is, how json/ is built, and how to cite.
