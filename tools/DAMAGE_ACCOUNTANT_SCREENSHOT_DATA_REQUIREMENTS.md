| Module | Required input | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Fire attribution | Fire detections, emissions, weather, transport model | Estimated fire contribution to PM2.5 |
| Exposure engine | PM2.5 grids, population, time | Population-weighted exposure |
| Health impact engine | Exposure, baseline health rates, risk functions | Expected attributable health outcomes |
| Economic engine | Health outcomes, work disruption, unit costs, valuation assumptions | Physical, financial and welfare accounts |
| Prevention evaluator | Intervention effectiveness, implementation cost, modeled exposure reductions | Avoided damage, BCR, net social benefit |
| Evidence and uncertainty layer | Source data, model versions, parameter ranges | Provenance, sensitivities and confidence limitations |
