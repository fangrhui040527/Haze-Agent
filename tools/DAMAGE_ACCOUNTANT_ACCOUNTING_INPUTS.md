| Data | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Scope | Haze event/source, countries, population groups, dates, comparison baseline, currency and price year |
| Physical harm | Attributable deaths, illness episodes, lost work, treatment or protective resources—each with a quantity, unit and scenario |
| Unit values | Cost per treatment, value per statistical death, production loss per unit, etc., with compatible currency/year and valuation assumptions |
| Evidence | Sources, estimation methods, uncertainty and limits on where the estimates apply |
| Coverage and exclusions | Categories included, missing or unestimated; overlapping costs that must be excluded |
| Financing, if available | Who pays whom, amounts, financing shares and transfers |
| Intervention scenarios, if requested | Harm with/without the project, project costs, effectiveness, adoption and maintenance assumptions |
