## 2. Small shared structures and invariants

Reuse the TypeScript/LangGraph/Zod stack, existing domain envelopes and versioned canonical serializer. Supplement the envelopes with immutable records, rather than embedding editable free text or inventing replacement planning/result formats. Every nonnull reference is `{id, revision, sha256}` and resolves to exact bytes. Every local reference begins `demo:`. Package identity also binds `programme_id` and `problem_id`; plan/activity/worker/location/scenario IDs remain stable across interfaces. Reuse a revision only for identical bytes. Changing content requires a new immutable reference; never mutate an already consumed artifact in a running trace.

The executable supplemental versions are `haze.evidence.v2`, `haze.scenario.v2`, `haze.decision_policy.v2`, `haze.demo_snapshot.v1`, `haze.input_draft.v1`, `haze.planner.input.v2`, `haze.calculation.v2`, `haze.independent_check.v2` and `haze.workershield.input.v2`. They are bounded engineering schemas: evidence accepts only `synthetic_fixture`, policy only fictional rules, and snapshots only historical/nonaccepted state. A snapshot embeds the existing planning shape plus `record_state=demo_local_snapshot`, `acceptance=not_canonically_accepted`, `proposal_status=pending_human_review` and disabled publication/eligibility. A draft carries partial proposed fields, field diagnostics and `accepted_bundle_ref=null`.

Full occupancy applies to every worker over the entire common horizon, with no gaps, overlaps, overtime or work deferred beyond it. Include real breaks/travel/setup/waiting where applicable; the fictional no-travel basis is confined to this fixture. Baseline and candidates pass the same allowed-start, release/deadline, precedence, worker availability/eligibility/nonoverlap, resource capacity, service and fixed-work predicates. Unsupported applicable constraints block the case; unknown applicability is incomplete input. Renaming, splitting, merging or reassigning work to evade matched-activity protection is prohibited.

Locators must identify exact material: data locators select a series/run, rows/intervals, locations and selection hash; structured locators identify record revision and field pointers; passage locators identify revision/page/section and exact passage offsets/hash. The local fixture binds its exact data rows and structured fields. A whole-report citation cannot support an individual parameter. Full operational provenance, source access and qualified permitted-use reviews remain future evidence requirements.

Worker and matched-worker/activity concentration-time totals must not increase in **any** required scenario. Crew totals alone are insufficient. Individual units are `hours * ug/m3`; crew units are `person-hours * ug/m3`. This proxy is not dose, safe duration, measured protection or health/damage benefit. Shared-activity movement is counted once: `M=sum(abs(candidate_start-baseline_start))`, `N=number changed`, `L=max movement`. Fixture caps are M<=360, N<=3, L<=120 minutes, with a 10% crew reduction floor in both scenarios. Qualifying candidates rank by `(M, N, L, -minimum_supported_crew_gain, -nominal_crew_gain, canonical_sequence_of(activity_id,start_UTC))`, using the pinned method's arithmetic precision. Canonical activity order and UTC start sequence resolve final ties. Any unknown implementation/coordination costs remain visible; fixed resources do not prove zero cost.

WorkerShield's required `investigation_refs` pins the actual `machine_report_ref` and `search_ref`, plus `ranking_ref` and `comparison_calculation_refs` when supported. This gives tools a typed, hash-bound route to ranking, cutoff and alternative calculations. A missing-evidence input has null ranking and no comparison calculations, with explicit reasons. References in free-text findings are not used as an interface.

`demo:generate` records the actual artifact-generation time and derives a fresh demo ID namespace from it. Source/decision clocks remain explicitly fictional and separate. `demo:check` replays the preserved `recorded_at`, making the same generation reproducible; deliberate regeneration produces new identities instead of rebinding an existing revision to new bytes. Its printed `package_sha256` can bind a human review to the exact canonical package bytes.

## 3. Drafts, accepted bundles and the historical fallback

An authorized controlled import/acceptance boundary would distinguish invalid, unsupported, incomplete/unattested drafts and accepted immutable bundles. A syntactically valid planning object with attestation references is not itself evidence that the attestations exist or are authorized. Present but unattested operating facts remain drafts; an accepted operating bundle may explicitly record environmental gaps and yield a completed insufficiency result. Do not turn missing operating facts into a fabricated baseline or pass an accepted-bundle agent interface invented references.

No such canonical importer/current head is established here. The local harness resolves explicitly fictional immutable `demo:` records; synthetic provenance and engineering checks are visible. Historical results have no canonical epoch/head/publication/currency receipt, no exact human approval and false eligibility. Local hash integrity is not canonical acceptance. Existing World Model facades/checkpoint stores are not substituted for the unproved v0.13 authority boundary. The default run validator rejects `demo:` bundle refs on the canonical surface.

Future canonical admission needs an explicit principal, programme/problem/artifact access, independent reviewer privileges, idempotency fingerprinting, current-bundle checks and actual conditional publication/current-read/event proof. These are implementation and evidence gates, not permissions supplied by this document.
