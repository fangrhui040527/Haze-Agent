# Damage reduction plan 05: reviewed external evaluation

Status: engineering path implemented; operational release remains gated on a reviewed non-synthetic reference trace. See the [implementation report](../../reports/damage-reduction/damage-reduction-05-implementation-report.md) for executed checks and limits.

Prerequisite: [pilot and delivery records](damage-reduction-04-pilot-delivery.md) and the earlier assessment, review and coordination contracts. This is the final stage of the [physical-endpoint roadmap](damage-reduction-planning-plan.md). Engineering fixtures may be implemented before real evaluation results exist; operational conclusions require the reviewed non-synthetic inputs below.

## Outcome and scope

Import a qualified external comparative evaluation of this programme's actual automated digital intervention, check what Haze can reproduce, and determine which conclusions its registered criterion supports. Bind the evaluated exposure-alert or work-schedule service to its exact rule and delivery history. A supported below-target or insufficient-evidence result is valid completion.

This stage completes the physical-endpoint evaluation lifecycle for automated digital delivery and may be deferred for the hackathon. It does not implement an internal causal estimator, infer effects from raw exposure data or enable monetary targets. Those extensions require separately scoped later plans. Apply the shared [qualification records](../../architecture/damage-reduction/damage-reduction-reference.md#versioned-records-and-qualification), [claim eligibility](../../architecture/damage-reduction/damage-reduction-reference.md#claim-eligibility) and [numerical rules](../../architecture/damage-reduction/damage-reduction-reference.md#exact-threshold-comparison).

## Ownership and required inputs

The scientific owner supplies the external design/result specification, original report and data references, independently reviewed expected outputs, applicability dossier, uncertainty interpretation and material limitations. The local operator records adoption as a human attestation; Haze does not authenticate external credentials.

The evaluation/domain lead owns import validation and the server claim predicate. DamageAccountant performs the registered numerical checks; EvidenceCritic independently checks exact products and qualification completeness. Arithmetic agreement and model agreement cannot grant scientific qualification. Composition supplies domain handlers and registered tools without moving domain policy into generic task code.

## Implementation

1. Add a versioned external-evaluation import/result contract containing origin, source method/version, design, endpoint/unit, population, period, comparative estimates, uncertainty interpretation, exact evidence and external review references. Retain imported originals. Reject unsupported required fields or return a precise gap; never synthesize missing causal or uncertainty inputs.
2. Bind the evaluation to the exact registered target/protocol, agreed rule versions, population, period and live digital-action receipts/deviations. Require the qualified design to account for material departures. Simulations cannot supply actual delivery. Studies of another delivery may support scenario applicability; they cannot establish this programme's achieved effect.
3. Reuse plan 01's exact comparison and independent reconstruction where its supplied residual quantities support them. The first path supports point estimates. A bound/probability criterion requires a registered method explicitly supporting that interpretation and its dependence assumptions, even when the result is externally calculated. Preserve unsupported uncertainty as a gap rather than substituting a point criterion.
4. Persist separate technical-review and scientific-qualification records. Record what Haze reproduced, what it inspected and what remains externally calculated. Review covers exact method, protocol, evidence and output versions. Unresolved material objections, conditional or withdrawn qualification, held evidence and stale dependencies block an unconditional target conclusion.
5. Enforce the claim predicate on the server and revalidate current support on use. A qualifying imported result says **external evaluation supports the registered target** with reproduction limits. A point comparison says **point estimate meets target**, with its uncertainty limits. Unknown registration timing or post-outcome target changes cannot pass a prespecified-target gate.
6. Add Evaluation and learning views to the programme page, linking the registered criterion, delivered actions, deviations, original external result, reviews and supported conclusion. Preserve the full lineage in history after revisions/restart. Retain reviewed lessons as artifacts; World Model acceptance and H-MEM publication remain separate integrations.

## Verification and completion

Check incompatible scope, another programme's delivery, design/method mismatch, unaccounted deviations, unsupported uncertainty, self-review, imported approval flags, stale/withdrawn qualification and missing registration evidence. Simulated/proposed actions, alert dispatch, schedule-change receipts and bare before/after differences cannot by themselves establish reduced exposure or damage.

Verify supported, below-target and insufficient-evidence paths, independent arithmetic, duplicate imports, source withdrawal, revision invalidation and fresh-process reconstruction of the complete lineage. Run focused contract/domain/API/frontend checks and applicable repository checks; record new evidence rather than inheriting earlier readiness claims.

Completion requires a complete non-synthetic reference trace using reviewed external evaluation inputs, honest provenance and reproducible checks within the admitted resource envelope. Fixture-only engineering remains explicitly labelled. The physical programme can then be released for the qualified method and claim types; broader readiness is not implied.
